Road attacks on the Mandera border corridor since Kenya entered Somalia

Kenyan troops crossed into Somalia in mid-October 2011. This map shows the roadside bombs and ambushes reported since then on the road along the border from Mandera through Arabia, Fino and Lafey to Elwak.

About this map

What it is. A list of roadside bombs, ambushes and attacks on vehicles on the Mandera–Arabia–Fino–Lafey–Elwak border road since October 2011, built from public news reports. It is an independent personal project.

What it is not. It is not a complete record: many incidents go unreported or were not found. It is not an official product of the Government of Kenya, RCMRD or any other organisation. It is not a security assessment or travel advice, and it should not be used to make safety or operational decisions.

Disclaimer. Dates, death tolls and locations are taken from news reports and may differ from official figures. Some locations are approximate and are marked as such. Attackers are described only as the reports describe them, usually "suspected al-Shabaab". Views are my own and not those of my employer. Corrections are welcome: mohamedabsiyee@gmail.com
